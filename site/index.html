Where is My Train

Live running status, PNR status, station boards and train schedules for Indian Railways, in one simple app.

What it does

  • Where your train is right now, and how late it is running
  • PNR and booking status
  • Arrivals and departures at a station
  • Coach position on the platform
  • Full schedules, and trains between two stations

How it works

No account and no sign-in. Your phone requests the public Indian Railways enquiry pages directly, the same pages you would open in a browser, and shows the answer in a cleaner layout. Your saved trains and searches stay on your phone.

Train information comes from Indian Railways (NTES, run by CRIS). It is best effort. Always check times and platforms at the station.
