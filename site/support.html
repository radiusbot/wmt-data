Common questions

Why does a train show no live status?

Live running information comes from Indian Railways' public NTES service. If NTES has no data for a train or date, or the service is down, the app shows the last status it received and when that was.

Why do I have to solve a captcha to add a PNR?

PNR status is only available from the official Indian Railways page, which asks for a captcha. The app opens that page for you. It never sees or stores your IRCTC login.

Where are my saved trips stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

I paid to remove ads. How do I restore it on a new phone?

Open Menu › Remove ads › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Google or Apple account you bought it with. To cancel the subscription, use your Google Play or App Store subscriptions settings.
