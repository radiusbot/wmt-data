This policy explains what the Where is My Train app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by [publisher name] ("we").

No accounts

The app has no sign-up, login or user account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we run no server that receives your searches.

Data stays on your phone

Your saved trains, recent searches, PNR numbers you enter, settings, and cached train and station lists are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Requests to Indian Railways

To show train information, your phone connects directly to public Indian Railways enquiry websites, such as enquiry.indianrail.gov.in (NTES) and indianrail.gov.in. These requests go from your phone to those sites, not through us. Like any website, those sites receive your IP address and the details of your request, and they may set cookies in the app's built-in browser view. Their own terms and privacy policies apply. When you look up a PNR, the PNR number is sent to Indian Railways to fetch its status.

App configuration

The app downloads small settings files (for example, which features are switched on) from radiusbot.com, hosted on GitHub Pages. These downloads contain no information about you, but GitHub, as the host, may log your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information as described in Google's data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK. This includes:

  • the device advertising ID (the Android advertising ID; on iPhone, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked)
  • IP address, which is used to estimate approximate location
  • app interactions with ads, such as views and taps, plus diagnostic and performance data

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.
