Where is My Train

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what the Where is My Train app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by RadiusBot ("we").

No accounts

The app has no sign-up, login or user account. The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and we run no server that receives your searches.

Data stays on your phone

Your saved trains, recent searches, PNR numbers you enter, settings, and cached train and station lists are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Requests to Indian Railways

To show train information, your phone connects directly to public Indian Railways enquiry websites, such as enquiry.indianrail.gov.in (NTES) and indianrail.gov.in. These requests go from your phone to those sites, not through us. Like any website, those sites receive your IP address and the details of your request, and they may set cookies in the app's built-in browser view. Their own terms and privacy policies apply. When you look up a PNR, the PNR number is sent to Indian Railways to fetch its status.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information as described in Google's data disclosures for the Mobile Ads SDK (Android, iOS). This includes:

  • the device advertising ID (the Android advertising ID; on iPhone, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked)
  • IP address, which is used to estimate approximate location
  • app interactions with ads, such as views and taps, plus diagnostic and performance data

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded, and asks again whenever Google requires it. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Menu › About › Privacy & terms › Ad privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when asked.

Feedback emails

If you use Feedback & report an issue, the app opens your own email app with a message addressed to our support inbox; nothing is sent until you press send. The email contains your email address (from your mail app), the topic and text you write, and, only if you leave Attach app & phone details switched on, the app version, phone model, operating-system version and free storage. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, never for marketing. We keep it only as long as needed for that, and you can ask us to delete your emails at any time by writing to radiusbot@gmail.com.

Purchases

Removing ads and optional tips are paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

No analytics or tracking by us

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of ours. We do not sell your information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date above. Questions: radiusbot@gmail.com.
